until it is confirmed as the lab's own and any credit is recorded.

## Standing rules
- No AI-generated *scientific* imagery — cells, mitochondria, experiments, hardware. A designed mark or
  an acknowledged montage is not covered by that rule.
- Nothing from the karanjin / K01 decks: the compound is under IP and the site masks it.
- Consent before publishing faces, names or personal links.
